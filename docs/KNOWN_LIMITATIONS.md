# Known limitations

- Source category labels can be inaccurate. For example, the Aite keyset `kk_3_1877_4562` is listed under AC upstream but contains decoder-style channel, EPG and digit commands. Its source category and button associations are retained; this is not evidence of AC compatibility.

- **IR ready** confirms Android detected a built-in consumer IR emitter; it does not prove the chosen codes work with an appliance. Carrier support may be unknown or exclude a profile's frequency. **IR check unavailable** reports a query error rather than hardware absence. **Check IR** sends no signal and cannot add hardware.
- Android APK only, Android 6.0 or newer. Most phones have no consumer IR emitter. No iPhone app or external USB, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi IR or RF hub is implemented.
- All bundled IR signals now come only from the v2.1 GitHub JSON archive of `ysard/mi_remote_database`. Previous IR datasets, the user-supplied Astro JSON, learned imports and the native stateful AC encoder are removed. Old IR devices must be added again from the new catalogue after updating; Wi-Fi devices and their credentials remain.
- Astro, NJOI, PRISM+ and PrismPlus were not found in the included source categories. There is no Astro Ultra Box / Ulti Box profile in this release. A profile for another decoder brand is not evidence of Astro compatibility.
- The catalogue is a pinned snapshot, not all models or all regions. Upstream notes that its queries were made for France/Europe. Source code-set identifiers are not verified retail model numbers. No physical TV, decoder, AC or fan testing was possible in the cloud.
- Only valid decoded raw signals are included. Unsupported carriers, empty responses and proprietary or executable AC definitions are excluded with reasons in `assets/ir/mi/index.json`. Upstream source totals therefore differ from the accepted app catalogue.
- AC and fan controls replay recorded commands only. AC commands may set multiple properties together. Arbitrary temperature/mode generation and appliance-state readings are unavailable. The app cannot synchronize a unit after another remote changes it.
- Original JSON button associations and keys are preserved, including repeated waveforms and unfamiliar commands. Display labels are derived from the source keys. There are no invented missing commands, inferred button aliases or automatic profile matches. The app does not learn IR through a phone's emitter and does not offer JSON import in this Mi-only build.
- Signals must have integer carriers from 20–60 kHz, 3–2,000 positive integer microsecond durations, no duration over one second and no total over 1.9 seconds. Unsupported signals are excluded intact, without truncating frames, guessing carriers or adding repeats.
- Samsung SmartThings needs existing manufacturer authorization, an appropriate token and internet. Tokens expire. There is no Palm account, subscription or shared token.
- LG local pairing uses the published legacy port-3000 SSAP interface on a trusted private LAN. Secure-only sets require a different implementation. Power-off is supported; wake behavior is model dependent.
- Development signing only; production store signing, Play publishing and an iOS build are separate tasks. English UI; Bahasa Malaysia translation is not included.
